## Three-minute interview sequence

| Time | Action | Point to make |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:35 | Speak a concise URL-shortener requirement, Stop, inspect/edit the transcript, then Generate. | Voice feeds the same proposal path as typed text; recording is sent only after Stop. |
| 0:35–1:05 | Inspect the draft's assumptions and connection directions, then Apply. | Apply is explicit; the proposal does not directly mutate the workspace. |
| 1:05–1:35 | Change one connection kind, Undo and Redo, then show the Click analytics boundary. | Canonical edits have ordinary history; grouping is explicit. |
| 1:35–2:00 | Open the prepared Design Brief and point to the queue tradeoff and unresolved traffic assumption. | User-authored reasoning is stored alongside the diagram but is not verified fact. |
| 2:00–2:40 | Open Analysis, then Grounded Review; expand Evidence on a useful question. | Local deterministic observations differ from advisory AI reasoning grounded in captured evidence. |
| 2:40–3:10 | Export PNG and Markdown, then show the downloaded files. | The diagram and brief travel as presentation artifacts; V5 JSON is the editable round-trip format. |

Avoid a menu tour. Give a provider response time to finish, but do not fill the entire demo with waiting. For an interview fallback, import the prepared JSON, make a semantic edit, show Analysis and the saved Brief, then open the prepared PNG and Markdown. If a recorded AI segment is available later, label it as a recording rather than suggesting it is a live response.
